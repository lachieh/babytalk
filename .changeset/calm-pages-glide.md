Moving between tabs is smoother: the header and bottom bar stay put, the page slides gently in the direction of the tab, and the tab marker glides across. Pages you have already seen open with what they showed last time and refresh quietly, and opening the app shows your baby and recent entries straight away while the latest is fetched. Placeholders shaped like the page only appear when there is genuinely nothing to show yet. The Feed, Sleep, Diaper and pump buttons wait until entries are known, so the side or amount they suggest is not a guess.
